X Native Extension

Normal paste

Hold Shift while pasting. Ctrl+Shift+V and Shift+Insert keep the browser's normal paste.

Or press Alt+Shift+V to make only the next composer paste normal. That also covers the right-click Paste menu.

Right-click a post

A photo offers download, open, and post for that image. A video or its poster offers the video versions. Text offers screenshot only. X’s own menu hides the browser menu, so these actions are drawn on the page. Post attaches the file for review and does not publish.

Shift+right-click keeps X’s menu. Alt+right-click opens the browser menu. Alt+Shift+S screenshots the post you just right-clicked.

Inserted text is one editing command, not hidden keystrokes. Details are on the options page.
